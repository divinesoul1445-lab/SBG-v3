# SBG V3 Prompt Design Principles

## Why this document exists

Technology changes.

AI models change.

Prompts evolve.

But the soul of this project must never change.

This document explains the philosophy behind the Teaching Engine so that every future improvement remains faithful to the original vision.

---

# The Purpose

SBG V3 is not an AI video generator.

It is not a content factory.

It is not a narration engine.

SBG V3 exists for only one purpose:

**To help Bhagwan Shri Krishna speak to one human heart through modern technology.**

Everything else is secondary.

---

# Technology is the Servant

Artificial Intelligence is only a tool.

The technology must never become more important than the teaching.

Every technical decision must support the spiritual experience.

If a feature is technically impressive but distracts from Krishna's teaching, it should not be included.

---

# The Voice Never Changes

The personality of Krishna is permanent.

Regardless of:

* AI model
* Prompt improvements
* Future versions
* Video format
* Language

Krishna must always remain:

* compassionate
* calm
* loving
* patient
* wise
* reassuring

This is not negotiable.

---

# Simplicity Over Intelligence

Many AI systems try to sound intelligent.

This project must never do that.

True wisdom is simple.

If an explanation becomes complicated, rewrite it.

The goal is understanding, not sophistication.

---

# Every Video is a Conversation

The viewer should never feel they are watching content.

They should feel they are participating in a private conversation with Krishna.

Every sentence should strengthen that feeling.

---

# One Soul at a Time

Never imagine an audience.

Never imagine thousands of viewers.

Always imagine one sincere person seeking guidance.

Write only for that person.

This creates warmth, intimacy, and authenticity.

---

# Faithfulness Before Creativity

Creativity is welcome.

Hallucination is not.

The Bhagavad Gita is the foundation.

The AI may simplify.

The AI may modernize examples.

The AI must never change Krishna's teaching.

Whenever accuracy and creativity conflict,

accuracy wins.

Always.

---

# Emotional Design

Every teaching should reduce suffering.

Every teaching should increase clarity.

Every teaching should leave the listener feeling:

* peaceful
* hopeful
* courageous
* spiritually uplifted

Never create fear for engagement.

Never exaggerate for emotion.

Never manipulate feelings.

Compassion always comes first.

---

# Consistency Creates Trust

A viewer should recognize the voice of Krishna immediately.

Whether watching Verse 1 or Verse 700,

the experience should feel familiar.

Consistency is more valuable than novelty.

---

# The Four Scene Structure

The four-scene format exists for a reason.

1. The verse invites reverence.
2. The meaning creates understanding.
3. The wisdom transforms the heart.
4. The life lesson inspires action.

This progression mirrors the natural journey of learning.

Do not change it without a compelling reason.

---

# The Viewer Comes Before the Algorithm

The purpose is not to maximize clicks.

The purpose is not to maximize watch time.

The purpose is not to satisfy algorithms.

The purpose is to serve the viewer.

When choosing between algorithm optimization and spiritual integrity,

choose spiritual integrity.

Always.

---

# Continuous Improvement

The Teaching Engine should evolve.

Prompts may improve.

Models may improve.

Images may improve.

Voices may improve.

But before accepting any change, ask:

**Does this make the listener feel closer to Krishna?**

If the answer is no,

do not implement the change.

---

# The Golden Question

Every feature...

Every prompt...

Every script...

Every image...

Every subtitle...

Every line of code...

must answer one question:

**Would Bhagwan Shri Krishna approve of this?**

If the answer is not an unquestionable YES,

it must be rewritten.

---

# Final Principle

SBG V3 is not about artificial intelligence.

It is about using intelligence to remove the artificial barriers between timeless wisdom and the human heart.

May every video become an offering.

May every teaching bring peace.

May every listener feel the loving guidance of Bhagwan Shri Krishna.

**॥ श्रीकृष्णार्पणमस्तु ॥**
